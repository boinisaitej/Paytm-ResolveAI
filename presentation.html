📌 Problem Statement

The Cost of Fragmented Resolution: Why Conversational Bots Fail in Fintech Operations

Traditional LLM wrappers and conversational FAQ bots collapse in fintech because financial workflows demand deterministic transactional actions, not generative text.

1

The Deflection Illusion

Traditional customer support bots prioritize ticket deflection over issue resolution, providing canned FAQ links without accessing core transactional ledgers. Customers remain stuck with unverified claims.

2

Operational Overhead

When deflection fails, tickets drop into manual queues where support agents spend 8–12 minutes toggling between 4 disparate internal portals (Core Banking switch, PG logs, Soundbox IoT telemetry, NPCI dispute portal).

3

Financial Risk & Hallucination

Unbounded generative LLMs hallucinate refund timelines or commit unauthorized payouts, while lacking PCI-DSS PII redaction and deterministic financial guardrails. This poses massive compliance liabilities.

4

The Metric Impact

Prolonged 48–72 hour dispute resolution turnaround times (TAT), heightened Customer Effort Score (CES), RBI T+1 turnaround SLA non-compliance penalties, and merchant terminal churn.
