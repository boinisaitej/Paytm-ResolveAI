📌 Problem Statement

The Scale Crisis in Digital Payment Operations

With over 350 Million users and 100M+ transactions daily, traditional support and payment ops collapse under transaction edge-cases.

48-72h Dispute Latency

Pending debits, bank timeouts, and U69/U30 gateway codes require manual ticket escalations across 3 disjointed support tiers, frustrating customers.

Fragmented Silos

Customer support, core banking analysts, merchant field engineers, and risk officers operate in isolated tools without unified transaction telemetry.

Merchant Churn & Hardware

Soundbox audio sync drops, SIM disconnections, and delayed settlements cause merchants to switch QR codes immediately, losing revenue.

The Cost of Inaction: Over ₹180 Crores spent annually on Level-1 repetitive triage, high Customer Effort Score (CES), and SLA penalties.
RBI Turnaround Requirement: T+1 Days
📌 Proposed Solution

Dual-Tier Workforce: 5 Human Roles + 5 AI Agents

An enterprise-grade collaboration model pairing specialized AI agents with authenticated human roles.

5 Human Application Roles (RBAC)

RBAC GATED
  • 1
    Customer: Voice/text multilingual dispute filing, live ticket tracking.
  • 2
    Support Executive: Assigned ticket triage, AI response draft reviews.
  • 3
    Payment Ops Analyst: Gateway code analysis (U69/U30), duplicate debits.
  • 4
    Merchant Ops Executive: Soundbox telemetry, settlements, device dispatch.
  • 5
    Supervisor / Admin: Final financial approval authority, AI workforce telemetry.

5 Autonomous AI Agents

AZURE OPENAI GPT-4O
  • 1
    Customer Support Agent: Multilingual STT, sentiment analysis, AI drafting.
  • 2
    Payment Investigation Agent: Core banking & gateway telemetry verification.
  • 3
    Dispute Resolution Agent: RBI/Paytm policy RAG matching & auto-reversal plan.
  • 4
    Merchant Operations Agent: Soundbox diagnostics, ledger sync & cross-sell.
  • 5
    Governance & Audit Agent: Risk scoring, SLA breach prediction, supervisor approval pack.
